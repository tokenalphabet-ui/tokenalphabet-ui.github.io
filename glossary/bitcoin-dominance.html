Bitcoin dominance

Bitcoin dominance is Bitcoin's market cap divided by the market cap of all cryptocurrencies, shown as a percent. If Bitcoin is worth $1.2 trillion and the whole market $2 trillion, dominance is 60%.

Traders watch it to see where money is flowing. Dominance tends to rise when altcoins fall faster than Bitcoin or when investors move into Bitcoin as the safer bet. A long slide in dominance is often read as the start of an altcoin rally.

Stablecoins complicate the picture. They are counted in the total market cap, so when people sell coins for stablecoins, Bitcoin dominance can drop even if Bitcoin itself holds up. Some analysts track dominance with stablecoins excluded for this reason.
