Stablecoin

A stablecoin is a token designed to keep a steady price, almost always $1. Traders use them to park money between trades without leaving crypto, and many people use them to send dollars across borders.

Most large stablecoins are backed by cash and short-term government bonds held by the issuer. USDT and USDC work this way. Others are backed by crypto deposited in smart contracts, like DAI and its successor USDS, and some use trading strategies to hold the peg.

The main risk is a depeg: the price slipping away from $1 because people doubt the reserves or the mechanism. Check who issues the coin, what backs it and whether the reserves are audited.
