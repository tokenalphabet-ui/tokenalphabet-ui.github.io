All-time high (ATH)

An all-time high, or ATH, is the highest price a coin has ever traded at. Its opposite is the all-time low, or ATL.

Price pages often show how far a coin is from its ATH, for example "-62% from ATH". That figure helps put today's price in context: a coin that has doubled this month may still be far below where it traded a few years ago.

Two sites can show slightly different ATH values for the same coin. Some record the very top of a short price spike on one exchange, others use an average across exchanges or daily closing prices.
