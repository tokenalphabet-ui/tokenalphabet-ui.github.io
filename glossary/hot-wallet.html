Hot wallet

A hot wallet keeps its private keys on a device that is online: a browser extension like MetaMask, a mobile app like Trust Wallet or Phantom, or a desktop app.

It is convenient. You can connect to apps, swap tokens and pay in seconds. The cost is exposure: malware, a fake browser extension or a malicious transaction you sign by mistake can drain it.

A common setup is to keep a small amount for daily use in a hot wallet and the rest in a cold wallet.
