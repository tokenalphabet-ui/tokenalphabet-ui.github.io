Fear & Greed index

The Fear & Greed index puts crypto market sentiment on a scale from 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed). Different providers build it from different inputs, usually some mix of volatility, price momentum, trading volume, social media activity and Bitcoin dominance.

Typical bands are 0 to 24 for extreme fear, 25 to 44 for fear, around 50 for neutral, 56 to 75 for greed and 76 to 100 for extreme greed. Exact cutoffs vary by provider.

Many people read it the other way round: extreme fear can mean coins are oversold, and extreme greed can mean the market is overheated. It is a mood gauge, though, not a timing tool. The index can sit in extreme fear for weeks while prices keep falling.
