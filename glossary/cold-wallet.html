Cold wallet

A cold wallet keeps private keys on a device that never exposes them to the internet. Most often it is a hardware wallet such as Ledger, Trezor or Keystone.

When you send coins, the transaction is prepared on your computer or phone, signed inside the device and only the signature goes back out. Malware on the computer cannot read the key.

It does not protect against everything. If you approve a bad transaction on the device screen, it will sign it. If someone finds your written seed phrase, the hardware does not matter. Always check the address and amount on the device itself before confirming.
