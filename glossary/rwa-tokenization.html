RWA tokenization

Real-world asset (RWA) tokenization means issuing a blockchain token that represents ownership of something outside crypto: government bonds, money market funds, private credit, gold or real estate.

Tokenized US Treasury funds are the biggest category so far. BlackRock's BUIDL fund and Franklin Templeton's on-chain money market fund are well-known examples, and Tether Gold (XAUT) tracks physical gold.

The token is only as good as the legal structure behind it. You rely on the issuer to hold the asset, honor redemptions and follow the rules of its jurisdiction. Many RWA products are open only to verified or professional investors.
