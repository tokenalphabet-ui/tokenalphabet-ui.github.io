Trading volume

Trading volume is the total value of a coin bought and sold over a period, usually the last 24 hours, added up across the exchanges a site tracks.

High volume means it is easy to buy or sell without moving the price much. Low volume means a single large order can push the price several percent. Some people compare volume to market cap: a coin with $1 billion market cap and $500 thousand daily volume is hard to trade in size.

Volume numbers are not always honest. Some exchanges have been caught trading with themselves to look busier, which is called wash trading. Data sites try to filter these markets out, but a sudden volume spike on one unknown exchange deserves a second look.
