Self-custody

Self-custody means you hold the private keys to your coins yourself, in your own wallet. The opposite is custodial storage: coins sit on an exchange, and the exchange holds the keys.

With self-custody nobody can freeze your funds or lose them in a bankruptcy, which is what happened to customers of several exchanges that collapsed. The phrase "not your keys, not your coins" comes from those cases.

The responsibility moves to you. There is no password reset: lose the seed phrase and the coins are gone for good. Good habits matter more than the choice of wallet.
