Circulating supply

Circulating supply is the number of coins that exist and can be traded right now. It is the figure used to calculate market cap.

Total supply counts all coins created so far minus any that were burned, including ones still locked with the team or investors. Max supply is the hard cap: Bitcoin will never have more than 21 million coins. Some coins, like Ethereum, have no max supply at all.

Watch unlock schedules. When locked tokens are released, circulating supply grows, and if demand does not grow with it, the price per coin tends to fall.
