Market cap

Market capitalization, or market cap, is the price of one coin multiplied by the number of coins in circulation. A token that trades at $2 with 500 million coins circulating has a market cap of $1 billion.

It is the number most sites use to rank coins, because price alone says nothing about size. A coin at $0.01 can be worth more in total than a coin at $500 if it has far more units out there.

You will also see fully diluted valuation (FDV). It uses the maximum supply instead of the circulating one, so it shows what the market cap would be if every coin that can ever exist were already trading. A big gap between market cap and FDV usually means many tokens are still locked and will be released later.

Market cap can mislead for small or thinly traded coins. If only a small share of the supply is actually for sale, a few trades can move the price and with it the whole market cap.
