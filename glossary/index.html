Crypto glossary

The words behind every crypto price page, explained in a few paragraphs each. For live numbers, see tokenalphabet.com.

  • All-time high (ATH): ATH is the highest price a coin has ever reached.
  • Bitcoin dominance: Bitcoin dominance is Bitcoin's share of the total crypto market cap.
  • Circulating supply: Circulating supply is the number of coins available on the market now.
  • Cold wallet: A cold wallet keeps private keys offline, usually on a hardware device.
  • Fear & Greed index: The crypto Fear & Greed index rates market sentiment from 0 to 100.
  • Hot wallet: A hot wallet keeps your keys on a device connected to the internet.
  • Market cap: Market cap is a coin's price multiplied by its circulating supply.
  • Private key: A private key is the secret number that controls a crypto address and signs its transactions..
  • RWA tokenization: RWA tokenization puts real-world assets like bonds, gold or real estate on a blockchain as tokens.
  • Seed phrase: A seed phrase is the 12 or 24 word backup of a crypto wallet.
  • Self-custody: Self-custody means holding your own private keys instead of leaving coins on an exchange.
  • Stablecoin: A stablecoin is a token built to hold a steady price, usually $1.
  • Trading volume: 24h trading volume is the dollar value of a coin traded in the last day.
