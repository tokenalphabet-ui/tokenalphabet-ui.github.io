Seed phrase

A seed phrase, also called a recovery phrase, is a list of 12 or 24 ordinary words that your wallet shows you when you create it. Every private key in the wallet is derived from these words.

Whoever has the phrase has the funds. With it you can restore the wallet on any device if your phone breaks or your hardware wallet gets lost. Anyone else who sees it can empty the wallet just as easily.

Write it down on paper or metal and keep it offline. Never type it into a website, a chat or a form, and never store it as a screenshot or in cloud notes. No real support team will ever ask for it.

Some wallets let you add an extra passphrase, sometimes called the 25th word. It creates a different wallet from the same seed, so a stolen phrase alone is not enough.
